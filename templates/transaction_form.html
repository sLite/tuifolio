01 Transaction

Use Staking reward only for genuine staking. Reward units have zero acquisition cost and no receipt-time income. Record no purchase payment. Current market value contributes to PnL. Do not use for lending or other non-staking Earn payments.

02 Asset

Enter a positive quantity. The transaction type determines whether it increases or decreases the balance.

Gifts and genuine staking rewards record zero acquisition cost automatically. No purchase payment is needed.

03 Quote and cash

Buy and Sell require a quote asset and amount. Asset increase and Asset decrease accept an optional quote for cost basis or proceeds. Omit it for valuation-only adjustments. Enter 0 for a known zero cost basis, or choose Gift to record an incoming asset with implicit zero cost.

Cost basis only changes PnL. Post cash movement also deducts cash for Buy or Asset increase, and adds cash for Sell or Asset decrease.

Fees, exchange and broker optional

Fees always deduct from the selected fee asset, regardless of transaction type or cash effect.
